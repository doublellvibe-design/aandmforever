Wamkelekile

You Are Welcome

With joyful hearts, the Mpondo family invites you to celebrate the ulwamkelo luka makoti — the sacred Xhosa tradition in which the bride is welcomed, embraced and accepted into her new home and family.

More than a ceremony, this is a gathering of two lineages. It is a moment to honour our ancestors, to share umqombothi, to dance the umxhentso, and to weave another thread into the living cloth of our people. We ask that you come with open hands and an open heart.

“Umntu ngumntu ngabantu.”
A person is a person through other persons.

The Meaning

Ulwamkelo means “the receiving” or “the welcoming.” Umakoti is the bride. Together, the phrase speaks to the gentle and deliberate way a Xhosa family receives a new daughter — not as a stranger, but as one of their own.

  • Ukubingelela — the greeting of guests
  • Ukungena — the bride enters the home
  • Ukubusiswa — the blessing of the union

Iimpahla

What to Wear

Guests are warmly invited to wear any traditional or cultural attire that celebrates who you are — there is no requirement to wear anything Xhosa-specific. Come in the dress, colours and garments of your own heritage and culture, or in smart, joyful attire of your choosing. We cannot wait to see the celebration in full colour.
